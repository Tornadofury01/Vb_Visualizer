# User guide

> **Coming soon.** The app is still early; this guide fills in as features ship.

VB Visualizer is a tool for coaches and players to draw volleyball plays on a 3D
court and watch them play out.

## Planned topics

- Moving around the 3D court — rotate, zoom, camera angles
- Dropping in players and tagging them by position and role
- Drawing ball paths and player routes
- Setting jump points on a route
- Scrubbing the timeline to check jump and route timing against the ball
- Following a player's point of view

## Available now

- Open the app and orbit/zoom the court. See
  [Getting started](/developer/getting-started) in the developer guide.
